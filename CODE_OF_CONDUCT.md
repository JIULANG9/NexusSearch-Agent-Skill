# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are people working on research tooling. Judgement applies to sources, evidence and
code — never to identity. Contributions are welcome regardless of experience level;
"this is my first PR to an AI skill repo" is a good reason to open one.

## Required

- Cite the source. When you claim something about another project, link the file,
  commit, paper or page you read. "I think most people do it this way" is not evidence.
- Argue with the artefact, not the person. Review comments target lines, not authors.
- Assume good faith for unmerged work, and take responsibility for your own merges.
- Keep the workspace private by default: never commit someone else's `runs/`,
  `Documentation/`, evidence or research input without their explicit say-so.
- Report in the open what a tool could not prove. Silent over-claiming is the failure
  mode this project exists to prevent, so we hold contributors to the same gate.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, gatekeeping, or mocking a question for being basic.
- Publishing generated "research" with fabricated citations, or dropping evidence that
  contradicts a preferred conclusion.
- Uploading a user's workspace content, credentials, or private repositories to any
  service to make a demo look better.
- Sustained off-topic behaviour in issues and PRs after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

Email the maintainer (address in `SECURITY.md`) or open an issue labelled
`conduct-report` if email is not possible. Reports are read by the maintainer only;
if a report concerns the maintainer, say so in the report and it will be handled
without them. Retaliation against a reporter is treated as a further violation.

## Consequences

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that violate this document, or
temporarily/permanently ban a contributor. Actions are proportionate and explained:
a first, low-impact slip gets a private note; fabricated evidence gets a public
correction and a removal. Decisions can be appealed by replying to the notice.
